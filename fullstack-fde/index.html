Live demo · grounded answers with evals

If the answer can't be proven, it doesn't ship.

A fictional US client: Ridgeline Supply, a mid-size HVAC distributor. Its knowledge base holds warranty, returns, shipping and product spec documents, and one outdated warranty policy that a newer one replaces. Pick a contractor's question. A Claude agent searches the docs, reads what it will cite, and has to pass an eval gate before it can publish.

Real agent, sandboxed data. Claude Haiku 4.5 on Amazon Bedrock with four tools: BM25 keyword search, read a chunk, an eval gate, and publish. The search and the evals are plain deterministic code. Ridgeline and its documents are made up; the reasoning is live.

Freshness is a rule, not a hope.Two warranty versions disagree. Search ranks the old one first. The gate fails any claim that relies on a superseded policy, or that uses the new one without the scope rule that proves it applies.

What I do now

I turn business processes into agentic architecture.

Your posting lists the decomposition I already run in every discovery. Technology comes last.

1Outcome

What it's worth, why now, and what happens if nothing changes.

2Process

The current state, step by step, from the people who run it.

3Decisions

Where judgment happens, and who is accountable for it.

4Data

What exists, who owns it, how fresh it is, and what's missing.

5Systems

CRM, warehouse, ticketing, messaging: what the agent reads and writes.

6Actors

Who escalates to whom, and where a human stays in the loop.

7Constraints

Security, compliance, budget, politics. The month-two killers, found in week one.

Then I choose the smallest intervention that works.

I start on the left and only move right when the step before can't do the job. The trade-off goes in an ADR the client signs off on.
