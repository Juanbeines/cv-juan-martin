05 First two weeks

      
Four weeks to ramp up? Give me two.

      
A new lead should earn the team's trust before changing anything. So the first week is listening and shadowing, and the second week is the first shared templates.

    

    
Day 1Day 10 · end of week two

    

    

      
Days 1–2
Meet the team
1:1 with every CSA: their accounts, what blocks them, what they want to grow into. Build a small app on Supabase myself so I speak the platform.
A map of people, accounts and risks

      
Days 3–5
Shadow every CSA
Join one live call per CSA and one escalation. Review open discoveries and proofs-of-value with Sales, plus the pipeline data in HubSpot and BigQuery.
Where proofs stall, and why

      
Days 6–8
Standardize the basics
Ship v1 templates with the team: discovery brief, proof-of-value scope with success criteria, onboarding email sequence.
Shared templates in use

      
Days 9–10
Coaching and hiring
A development plan for each CSA (storytelling, Postgres tuning, account plans), a hiring scorecard, and the first conversion metrics we'll track.
