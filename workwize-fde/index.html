Live demo · an onboarding agent

A messy HRIS export. Day-one laptops anyway.

The kind of thing I'd build in week one with a new US client. Pick a client's export. The agent reads it, checks stock and lead times per country, drafts every laptop order and retrieval, and a validator rejects anything wrong: late deliveries, PO boxes, duplicates. The agent fixes what the rules allow and hands the rest to a human, with the reason.

Real agent, sandboxed data. Claude Haiku 4.5 on Amazon Bedrock, calling seven tools against a made-up catalog, stock list and asset register. The clients, people and addresses are fictional, and this is not Workwize's API. The reasoning is live.
